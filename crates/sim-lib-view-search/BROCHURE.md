# Inspect every search claim

`sim-lib-view-search` makes the whole evidence chain visible on compact,
tablet, and desktop surfaces. A reviewer can expand a result to inspect its
rank contributions, provider claims, fetch and robots decisions, immutable raw
capture, normalized representation, exact selector, fidelity warnings, policy
receipts, optional judge receipt, and office anchor—fully offline.

Provider snippets are unmistakably unverified. Only selector-checked captured
characters receive quotation styling. Remote strings stay inert until a user
asks a policy host to open one.
